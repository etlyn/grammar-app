# Spanish course coverage

Version: spanish-2026-11-v1

An initial Spanish grammar course with English explanations and original Spanish examples and practice. Approximate A1–B2 teaching labels are not a complete CEFR syllabus or a calibrated assessment. General shared usage is taught with explicit regional and register qualifications. Educator review is pending.

30 chapters · 120 reading sections · 720 original exercises · six stages · ten map areas. Each chapter has four skills and 24 exercises, six per skill. A session draws 20 without replacement.

Content SHA-256: e622b914ebf35fb2b850380275ea0207447139ee09abdb3f388ae199562b59ce

All explanations, examples and exercises are original and authored offline with AI assistance. References inform scope or grammar guidance; no publisher exercises are reproduced or adapted. Independent educator/native-speaker review is pending. Levels are approximate teaching labels; this grammar course does not cover all CEFR communication skills or certify a level.
